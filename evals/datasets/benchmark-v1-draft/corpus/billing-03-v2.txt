# BILLING-03: Usage accounting contract / revision 2

A single request may reserve at most 9 cents of provider cost.
The workspace's daily provider budget is 5 dollars.
Usage ledger records are retained for 50 days.
Daily budget windows begin at midnight UTC.
If execution may have occurred but usage is unknown, charge the full reserved estimate.
Cached input tokens are a subset of total input tokens and are not added twice.
