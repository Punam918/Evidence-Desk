# INCIDENT-03: Incident response policy / revision 1

A severity-one alert must be acknowledged within 11 minutes.
If the primary responder is unavailable, escalate to the secondary after 5 minutes.
Incident timelines are retained for 50 days.
The incident commander owns customer-facing status updates.
Declare recovery only after the error-rate alarm is clear for ten consecutive minutes.
The post-incident review records contributing conditions without assigning personal blame.
