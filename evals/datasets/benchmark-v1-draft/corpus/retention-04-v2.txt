# RETENTION-04: Document lifecycle policy / revision 2

Completed conversation messages expire after 11 days.
Private originals are physically purged within 6 hours after a deletion request.
Encrypted recovery backups are retained for 60 days.
A deletion tombstone blocks new source reads immediately, before physical erasure completes.
Saved answers referencing a deleted source are hidden on the next authorized read.
A restore replays independent deletion records before accepting application traffic.
