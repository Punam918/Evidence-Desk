# RATE_LIMIT-04: Request admission protocol / revision 1

An authenticated actor may submit at most 13 mutation requests per minute.
At most 6 generation jobs may run concurrently in one workspace.
Idempotency records remain valid for 60 hours.
A reused idempotency key with a changed body returns a conflict instead of starting new work.
Capacity rejection occurs before provider transport or cost reservation.
Retry-After expresses the earliest safe retry delay in whole seconds.
