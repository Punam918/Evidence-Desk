# Project selection: EvidenceDesk

## Concepts compared

Scores 1–5 are explicit design judgments, not market research. Higher is better, including affordability and feasibility. Equal weighting prevents a spurious precision claim; interview appeal is assessed by visible engineering work, not employer surveys.

| Broad enterprise knowledge intelligence | 5 | 5 | 3 | 2 | 4 | 3 | 4 | 26 |
| Multi-tenant engineering knowledge workspace | 5 | 5 | 4 | 4 | 5 | 4 | 5 | 32 |

Broad enterprise systems imply many connectors, ACL inheritance and governance features. Research analysis has strong retrieval depth but difficult correctness labels for novel synthesis. Code intelligence adds parsing/repository version issues. Support automation needs integrations and consequential actions. General workflows make task success and safe permissions too open-ended.

**Select EvidenceDesk: an evidence-first knowledge workspace for small engineering and technical-support teams.** Users upload runbooks, release notes and product policies; ask grounded questions; compare two document revisions; inspect citations and generation cost; save a reviewed brief. Workspace isolation, source lifecycle and paired retrieval experiments create demonstrable engineering depth.

## Users and value

- Support engineer: answer a product-policy question with a verifiable source and version.
- Engineering lead: compare runbook revisions before approving a support brief.
- Workspace owner: control membership, local-only processing and spend; diagnose ingestion failures.
- Portfolio reviewer: reproduce experiments, inspect limits and observe recovery without private data.

Differentiation is a visible evidence inspector, explicit abstention, version-aware comparison, enforceable privacy routing, and honest retrieval/cost reports. None is claimed unique in the market. Useful daily workflows, measurable correctness and feasible scope matter more than novelty claims.

## Scope boundaries

Core release includes one workspace-level document visibility boundary, private per-user conversations, English text extraction, paid and local inference, hybrid retrieval, citations, constrained comparison and approved brief saving. Documents are visible to all current workspace members; there are no per-document ACLs in v1. Document deletion is owner/admin only; conversation deletion is creator only. This is an explicit product decision, not an authorization omission.

Mandatory hardening includes malicious-file handling, durable leases, stream replay, deletion, budgets, regression/evidence checks, backup restoration and honest operational targets. Stretch: OCR, multilingual embeddings, external connectors, third/fourth provider polish, GPU automation, persistent personal memory and Kubernetes. External email/ticket publishing and arbitrary code/URL tools are out of scope.

Estimated implementation: 14–18 focused full-time weeks (560–720 hours), or roughly 7–11 months at 15–20 hours/week, including contingency. This is a planning assumption. Scope can be reduced to the stage-3 baseline without removing tenant security, deletion or failure transparency. Interview value comes from verified quality and tradeoffs, not implementing every optional technology.
